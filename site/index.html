What it does

Everything your car did, made legible.

Why EVBook

Thorough like a server. Effortless like a service.

TeslaMate proved a self-hosted logger can be gentle to the battery and thorough with the history. Tessie showed how effortless the experience can be. EVBook is an attempt at both, on infrastructure you do not have to operate.

Deploy

Your own, in four commands.*

* or one single prompt

The deploy provisions the D1 database and the R2 bucket by name in your account, and the worker applies its own migrations — so the same unmodified configuration deploys into any Cloudflare account.

evbook — zsh
$ npm install
$ npx cf auth login
# asks for the password you will log in with,
# generates the encryption key, and deploys
$ npm run setup

Prefer a server? A VPS with Docker works too — one container, one SQLite file.

  1. Or let an agent do it Point an AI agentClaude, Codex, Antigravity or any other modern AI agent that supports Agent Skills. at the bundled evbook-deploy skill and ask it to deploy. It provisions, migrates, sets secrets and verifies — without ever asking you to paste a secret into the chat.
  2. Connect your car Open the URL, log in, and paste a Tesla access + refresh token pair — our Token Helper extension for Chrome gets you one in a single sign-in. Your cars appear within seconds.
  3. Keep your encryption key safe It encrypts your Tesla tokens at rest. Losing it means signing in to Tesla again — your logged history is never affected.
